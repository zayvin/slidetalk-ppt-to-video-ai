# English and Mandarin voiceover scripts

Use either language for one complete take. The on-screen slides are in English. Times are pacing targets for a 40-second lesson. Move slide changes and subtitle cues to match the finished audio.

## Slide 1 · Newsletter signups

**00:00–00:10** · Read both bars and the denominator. Leave a short pause after the second week.

**English**

A hundred visitors came each week. How many signed up? Twenty signed up in week one. Thirty signed up in week two.

**普通话**

两周各有一百人访问。有多少人订阅了电子报？第一周有二十人订阅，第二周有三十人。

## Slide 2 · Percentage-point change

**00:10–00:20** · Point to the two rates in order, then pause on the unit.

**English**

The rate went from twenty percent to thirty percent. Subtract the two rates. The gap is ten percentage points.

**普通话**

订阅率从百分之二十升到了百分之三十。把两个比例相减。订阅率上升了十个百分点。

## Slide 3 · Relative change

**00:20–00:30** · Stress the starting rate. Give listeners time to read the denominator.

**English**

How big is that gain compared with the starting rate? Ten divided by twenty is one half. That's a fifty percent increase.

**普通话**

和原来的订阅率相比，这次的增幅有多大？十除以二十，等于二分之一。也就是说，相对增幅是百分之五十。

## Slide 4 · One sentence for the voiceover

**00:30–00:40** · Speak the final sentence once as you would say it in a report.

**English**

Our signup rate rose from twenty to thirty percent. That's ten percentage points higher, a fifty percent increase on week one.

**普通话**

我们的订阅率从百分之二十升到了百分之三十。一共上升了十个百分点，比第一周提高了百分之五十。
