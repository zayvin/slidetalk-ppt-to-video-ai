# How to convert PowerPoint to MP4 with narration and subtitles

On Mac, iPhone or iPad, start with one slide that needs an explanation. A good test is a chart or a screenshot where the viewer could easily look at the wrong thing. If the voice, highlight and subtitle all direct attention to the right place, use the same approach for the rest of the deck.

## Make a one-slide test

1. **Choose the input.** Open your PPTX in SlideTalk. Compare its busiest slide with the original. If labels have shifted or a diagram looks different, export the same slide deck as a PDF in PowerPoint or Keynote and import that copy. For an old `.ppt` file, save a new PPTX or PDF first.
2. **Give the narrator a job.** For a demonstration of password recovery, try “Guide a first-time user through the recovery screen. Say the button label and tell them how to recognize success.” Replace generic phrases in the draft with the words actually visible on your slide.
3. **Listen with the screen covered.** A listener should know what to do and what to expect. Then uncover the screen and preview the moment the voice names the button. Place the emphasis while the button is visible. Check the voice available for your target language with a short sentence containing a name or abbreviation.
4. **Export and check the MP4.** Watch it muted with captions at phone size. Then play it with the screen covered and listen for any instruction that depends on seeing the slide. If you publish a selectable subtitle track, test the SRT alongside the final video and turn captions on and off in the destination player.

## An example you can copy

| On the slide | First draft | A line you can record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Recovery email” field and a “Send link” button | “Enter the information and proceed.” | “Enter the email you used to sign up. Select **Send link**.” |
| Confirmation screen showing a masked address | “The process is complete.” | “Check the address on this screen. If the last letters match yours, open that inbox and look for the recovery email.” |

Hold the second screen until the narrator finishes saying what to check. If the flow in your product uses different labels, replace both the screenshot and its spoken instruction.

## Can I use a PDF or keep the slides offline?

A PDF is useful when the page layout matters more than editable objects. Build out progressive reveals as separate pages before exporting the PDF. SlideTalk renders the finished video locally.
