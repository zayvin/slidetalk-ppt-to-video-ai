# Narrated presentation video review sheet

Choose one slide with small labels, one with numbers and the final slide. Run the quick checks on those first, then watch the full exported file. Mark each item after testing it in the intended player.

| Pass | Do this | A passing result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Import | Compare the busiest slide against the original at presentation size. | Font, units, legend and plotted values line up. |
| 2 · Listen | Cover the screen and listen to a slide with a date, acronym or percentage. | The voice names the unit and comparison period clearly. |
| 3 · Watch silently | Mute the video and view it on a phone. | The subtitle stays readable and leaves the relevant label visible. |
| 4 · Sync | Replay the last phrase before a slide cut and the first after it. | Each phrase belongs to the slide on screen. |
| 5 · Caption track | Toggle captions in the destination player. | One readable set appears in the chosen viewing mode. |
| 6 · Final frame | Play the last five seconds of the exported MP4. | The last word is complete and the final visual remains long enough to read. |
